== timing.puml: PlantUML / Timing (Timing diagram)
== lint
== outline
   2 robust | Network
   3 concise | Battery
   4 clock | Tick
   5 @ | 0
   8 @ | 10 (:charged)
  10 @ | 20
== fmt
@startuml
robust "Network" as NET
concise "Battery" as BAT
clock "Tick" as TICK with period 10
@0
NET is Idle
BAT is Full
@10 as :charged
NET is Busy
@20
BAT is Low
@enduml
