New diagrams drawn natively, films made to the size and the look a page or a
post wants, and what a tester's round and a research run found.

## Drawn natively

- **PlantUML's charts** (`@startchart`): bars side by side or stacked, lines,
  areas and points over `h-axis`, `v-axis` and `v2-axis`, horizontal too,
  with legends, labels and annotations, and `<style>` on the chart and its
  series. What hangs or fails the Java PlantUML is a mistake on its line.
- **Mermaid's marble diagrams** (`marble-beta`, proposed in
  mermaid-js/mermaid#8864), read as the proposal's reference reads them:
  lanes in RxJS's marble syntax lined up from one origin, groups, spans,
  breaks, an axis and labels.
- **Association classes** (`(Student, Course) .. Enrollment`): the line
  between the pair runs through its middle, and the class's dashed line
  starts there, as PlantUML lays it. Before, a use case called
  "Student, Course" was drawn and lint said nothing.
- **Gradients in the structure diagrams**: a box's own (`class Foo
  #red-green`), a skinparam's, the page's and a note's.

## Films

- **Every film in the look asked for**: `--look light`, `dark`, `plain`,
  `vivid`, `sketch` or `file` around the diagram, for a play, a fold, a
  dive, a lockstep, a history, a time travel, a build and traffic alike.
- **The size asked for**: `--size 1080x1920` lays the film out as wide as
  the frame lets it and sets it in the middle.
- **The bar** is as large as the diagram's words, has no tools in a film,
  and `--no-bar` leaves it out.
- **A build** leaves lines of comments out of the source it shows, and
  `--no-code` builds the diagram alone. A Salt window builds row by row;
  a picture whose lines tie no words comes whole with its `@end…` line,
  no longer cut at its foot.
- **The veil** lets a note through with its state, and goes once a
  scenario's last message is shown: its lifelines' feet no longer stay
  veiled, in the pane, a film, a presentation and a lockstep page.
- **A start's label is its event**: with `[*] --> Idle : power`,
  `play --events power,work` and a trace whose first line is
  `{"event":"power"}` both take it as the machine's start.

## For agents and pages

- `omaspec inspect` tells the behavior beside the outline: a machine's
  states and transitions, a scenario's participants, messages and notes,
  each on its line; the MCP server's `inspect` too.
- `render --id-prefix door-` gives a picture's ids a prefix of their own,
  so several stand on one HTML page.
- [ADR 0037](https://github.com/enhapu-afk/omaspec/blob/main/docs/adr/0037-an-id-for-every-box-and-line.md)
  decides the next step: an id from the source for every box and every line
  (`state-Idle`, `transition-Idle-Busy-start`), the key `diff` and `trace`
  know a transition by.

## Mermaid

- A flowchart's `padding`, `diagramPadding`, `nodeSpacing` and
  `rankSpacing` are carried out; `layout: elk` asks for Omaspec's own
  layout, which lays out in ranks with lines at right angles as ELK does.
- A diagram's title is a quarter larger than its text, as Mermaid sets it.

## Fixes

- A chart with a line that starts as a Mermaid kind does (`pie`, `block`)
  was read as Mermaid, even in a `.puml` file.
- A state moved across a concurrent region is a mistake now, which the Java
  PlantUML refuses too; the message said only Java draws it.
- Spaces widen a Salt button, `[Ok        ]`, as PlantUML's, its words in
  its middle; beside a block a row's other cells stand at its top.
- Subclasses written parent first (`Shape <|-- Circle`) share one triangle.
