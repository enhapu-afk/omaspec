Sequence, activity and state diagrams and flowcharts drawn by Omaspec's own
engines in PlantUML and Mermaid alike, and a landing page that shows every
function at https://enhapu-afk.github.io/omaspec/.

- **Show it.** Shift+F5 presents a diagram full screen, the speaker's notes in
  the editor's window; `omaspec render door.puml -o talk.html` hands the talk
  on as one page that plays offline. `omaspec play` writes films and pages in
  vectors: a diagram building itself from its source, a scenario in lockstep
  with the machines of its participants, a file's history commit by commit,
  recorded traces flowing through a machine, a sweep through the themes, from
  sketch to ink.
- **A project as one page.** `omaspec export DIR --html` writes the map and
  every diagram into one file; a click zooms into a tile as the editor does.
- **More views of a machine.** Its table of states and events
  (`omaspec matrix`), its life cycle on a ring (`omaspec orbit`), its coverage
  as a trace river (`omaspec coverage --river`).
- **Diagrams only Omaspec draws.** Story maps (`@startstorymap`) and
  capability maps (`@startcapabilities`), beside signal flow.
- **Long diagrams stay readable.** A sequence diagram's participants stay on
  top as pills while it scrolls; a click on a word in the picture lands on its
  line.
- **A guide.** Every function with examples, as a
  [PDF](https://enhapu-afk.github.io/omaspec/guide/omaspec-guide.pdf).

## Install

The archives below hold the binary for x86_64 and aarch64; it runs on glibc
2.35 and newer (Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Arch). Or build it:
`cargo build --release -p omaspec-app`.
